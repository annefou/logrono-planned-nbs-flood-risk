# REACHOUT's planned nature-based solutions and pluvial flood risk in Logroño

> **Do REACHOUT's planned nature-based solutions reduce building-level pluvial flood risk in Logroño (Spain)?** — one FORRT nanopublication chain, built on the [Logroño flood-adaptation scenarios analysis v0.1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23298913).

**Finding:** *The nature-based solutions of REACHOUT's planned scenario for Logroño reduce the number of residents in buildings at pluvial flood risk by less than 4 %, with no reduction during the 100- and 200-year storms.*

![Headline figure](figures/main_result.png)

The notebooks: (1) the analysis release and the REACHOUT maps, (2) the inputs of this finding, (3) the finding in every case, (4) the figure. The chain is listed in [`nanopubs/PUBLISHED.md`](nanopubs/PUBLISHED.md) once published.
