# Published nanopub chain — URI registry

This file is the canonical registry of published nanopub URIs for this chain: *Do REACHOUT's planned nature-based solutions reduce building-level pluvial flood risk in Logroño?* (question-rooted, built on the [Logroño flood-adaptation scenarios analysis v0.1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23298913); outcome repository: release v0.1.0, [10.5281/zenodo.23300575](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23300575)).

**Finding (AIDA):** *The nature-based solutions of REACHOUT's planned scenario for Logroño reduce the number of residents in buildings at pluvial flood risk by less than 4 %, with no reduction during the 100- and 200-year storms.* Outcome: validated, moderate confidence.

## Format

If a URI is not in the Science Live namespace, view it via the Science Live viewer by wrapping the URI:
